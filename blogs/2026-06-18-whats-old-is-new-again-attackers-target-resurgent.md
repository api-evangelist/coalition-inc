Coalition's Zero-Day Alerts analysis finds organizations are patching more slowly, opening opportunities for attackers to exploit both new and old vulnerabilities. The new median time to patch is nearly two weeks longer than it was in 2024, and vulnerability exploitation now accounts for 31% of all breaches.
